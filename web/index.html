About this map

Built from your installed snapshot. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How accurate is it?

  • Found worlds: structures are checked with Minecraft’s own generator and each reported one is fully built to confirm it. Coordinates are the locate position; Y is the bounding-box minimum.
  • Structure layers on the map use the same check the game’s /locate uses. Click a marker to build that structure and see its footprint.
  • Biomes are sampled every 32 blocks, then terrain height and biome boundaries are checked. Small patches may be missed.
  • The map is base terrain: trees, structures, decorations and later terrain changes are not drawn.
  • Spawn is the generator’s starting region, so the final player position can shift.
  • Default vanilla Overworld only. Custom world-generation datapacks are not included.
